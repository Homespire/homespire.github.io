In-House, Non-QM Product Suite

Bank Statement
  • Self employed, independent contractors or tip/service industry
  • 12 or 24 months of statements utilizing personal or business
  • Multiple expense ratios: Uniform (50%), Profit and loss, CPA verified expense ratio
  • 1099 program supplemental income
Asset Qualifier
  • No DTI, employment, or income required
  • All occupancies
  • Qualify based on liquid or near-liquid assets
  • 3 different qualification methods
  • 6 months of statements for each account being used
  • Recoup funds from a cash purchase as a rate and term
Investor Cash Flow
  • No DTI, employment, or income required
  • Qualify on Debt Service Coverage Ratio only (DSCR)
  • Interest-only available
  • Exempt from QM, ATR and HMPL requirement
  • Financing contributions up to 3%
Plus:
  • In-house Underwriting Support and Scenario Desk
  • Non-Warrantable Condos
  • Asset-Based Alt-Doc programs
  • LLCs allowed on Investment Properties

Medical Professionals

  • 7- and 10-year ARMs
  • Up to 95% LTV with no MI
  • Loan amounts up to $2,000,000

Bridge Loans

  • Use current equity to buy a new home before selling the old one
  • Allows buyers to make a non-contingent offer on a new home
  • Builds in funds for preparing the old house for sale
  • Covers payment on old mortgage for up to 6 months until it sells
